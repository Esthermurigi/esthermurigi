About Me

I'm Esther Murigi, an information security professional who helps organizations protect their systems, their data, and the people behind them.

My work is simple to describe and hard to do well: find the risks that actually matter, close them before they are exploited, and build security that supports the business instead of slowing it down. I translate technical findings into clear decisions leadership can act on — with the evidence to back them up.

Whether I'm embedded with an in-house team or advising as an independent consultant, the goal is the same: stronger defenses, fewer surprises, and the confidence to move fast without breaking things.

What I Do

  • Risk Assessment Identifying, scoring, and prioritizing the exposures that carry real business impact.
  • Security Hardening Reviewing configurations, access controls, and architecture to close gaps before they are found for you.
  • Incident Readiness Building the plans, playbooks, and response discipline that turn a crisis into a managed event.
  • Team Enablement Equipping technical and non-technical teams to make secure decisions by default.
